Notes

Things that went wrong while building a scraper people pay for, and what the fix turned out to be. Mostly the parts I had backwards.

  • A 403 on robots.txt means you may crawl. A 500 means you may not.

    6 October 2026

    RFC 9309 reads backwards until you check it, and I shipped a docstring claiming compliance with both status-code rules inverted.

  • A sold item does not 404

    6 October 2026

    It redirects to the home page, and your scraper reads a stranger's price. Three runs on one dead listing gave three different plausible numbers.

  • Never bill in a finally block

    6 October 2026

    Two failed calls charged a customer for work that never happened. The bug was four characters of control flow and it took a month to notice.
